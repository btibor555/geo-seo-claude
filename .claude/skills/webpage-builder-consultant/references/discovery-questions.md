# Discovery Question Set — Client Without a Website

Ask in rounds. Diagnose first, pitch later. Each question's *why* tells you which
page element it feeds. Add the industry block from `industry-playbooks.md`.

## Round 1 — Business & Offer (→ headline, services, main action)

1. What does your business do, in one sentence, the way a customer would say it?
2. What is the ONE service you most want more customers for? Why that one (margin, capacity, season)?
3. What cities/ZIPs/neighborhoods do you serve? Where do you NOT want jobs from?
4. Is there a specific offer to lead with (free estimate, inspection, consult, new-patient special, emergency service)?
5. What should a visitor do first: call, book online, request a quote, or fill an intake form?
6. Do you have capacity for more work right now? How many new jobs/clients/patients per week can you handle?

## Round 2 — Ideal Customer (→ copy, FAQ, qualification)

7. Describe your best customer. What problem are they dealing with when they find you?
8. What do they usually search or ask before they call?
9. What are the top 5 questions you answer on every call?
10. Why do people choose you over competitors? Why do they choose someone else?
11. Who is NOT a good fit (price shoppers, out-of-area, case types, insurance you don't take)?
12. Emergency/urgent vs planned work — what's the split?

## Round 3 — Trust Assets (→ proof, trust strip)

13. How many Google reviews and what rating? Other review sites (Yelp, Healthgrades, Avvo, Angi, BBB)?
14. Can we use real testimonials (with written permission)? Photos or video of customers?
15. Licenses, certifications, board/bar/manufacturer credentials, insurance, awards, associations?
16. Years in business, jobs completed, patients/clients served (only true numbers)?
17. Before/after photos or case examples we're allowed to show?
18. Photos of you, your team, trucks, office? (Real beats stock.)
19. Any guarantee, warranty, financing, or price-range you're comfortable publishing?

## Round 4 — Lead Capture & Follow-Up (→ form, CRM, automation)

20. Today, how do new leads reach you (calls, texts, Facebook, GBP, referrals)?
21. Who answers the phone? What happens after hours and when you miss a call?
22. Do you use a CRM, booking or field-service tool (GHL, ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, Clio, Dentrix/NexHealth, etc.)?
23. What info do you need to quote or book? What's the minimum?
24. How fast do you typically respond to a new inquiry? Who is responsible?
25. Should new leads get an instant text/email? A booking link? Reminders?
26. Do you ever lose leads or forget to call back? Roughly how many a month?

## Round 5 — Money & Goals (ask permission first) (→ value math, pricing, priority)

> "To see whether this is worth solving, can I ask a few questions about what a new customer is worth?"

27. What is one new customer worth (first job and lifetime)?
28. What percent of inquiries become paying customers?
29. How many new customers per month do you want from the website?
30. Do you run ads now or plan to? Monthly budget? Where would traffic come from?
31. Is getting a site live something you need now, or a future improvement? What's driving the timing?
32. What budget range have you set aside for this?

## Round 6 — Content & Brand (→ build inputs)

33. Business name, logo, brand colors, fonts (if any)?
34. Domain name — do you own one? Whose account is it in?
35. Phone, email, address (or service-area only), hours?
36. Sites you like (and why) — and competitors you want to beat?
37. Who will write/approve copy? Who approves the final site?
38. Do you need to edit the site yourself later?

## Round 7 — Compliance & Accounts (→ risk flags)

39. Does your industry restrict advertising claims (medical, legal, financial)?
40. Will the form collect any health, legal-case, or financial details? (Minimize.)
41. Do you need a privacy policy, disclaimers, accessibility statement, cookie consent?
42. Tracking: OK to install Google Analytics / Meta Pixel / call tracking?
43. Will accounts be in your name (domain, hosting, CRM, forms)? (Should be yes.)

## Stop signals (tell the owner plainly)

- Can't name one main service/offer → clarify offer before building.
- No traffic plan → site alone won't generate leads; pair with GBP/reviews/ads.
- No one owns follow-up → leads will die in the inbox; fix that first.
- No capacity → more leads will hurt reputation; fix operations first.
- Wants "just something pretty", no goal → not a fit, or reframe around main action.

## Blueprint mapping

| Answers | Feed into |
|---------|-----------|
| 1–6 | Headline, sub-copy, primary CTA, page type |
| 7–12 | Service copy, FAQ, form qualification, disqualifiers |
| 13–19 | Trust strip, proof section, risk reducers |
| 20–26 | Form spec, thank-you page, CRM + follow-up flow |
| 27–32 | Opportunity math, offer tier, price, pilot |
| 33–38 | Build inputs, tool choice (owner-editable?) |
| 39–43 | Compliance flags, tracking plan, account ownership |
