# Industry Playbooks — Target Verticals

Each block: main action · money page · must-have trust · form fields · follow-up ·
extra discovery questions · compliance flags. Use to specialize audits and blueprints.

## Family Doctors / Medical Clinics
- **Main action:** Book appointment (new patient) · secondary: call.
- **Money page:** "Accepting New Patients" page; per-service pages (physicals, chronic care, telehealth).
- **Trust:** Board certifications, provider bios + photos, insurance accepted list, hospital affiliations, Google rating.
- **Form:** Name, phone, email, new/existing, insurance carrier, preferred time. **No symptoms/health details** in non-HIPAA forms.
- **Follow-up:** Booking confirmation + reminders (cuts no-shows), intake link via patient portal.
- **Ask:** Accepting new patients? Which insurances? Same-day/walk-in? Telehealth? EHR/portal used?
- **Compliance:** HIPAA — BAA with form/chat/CRM vendors if PHI is collected; no Meta Pixel on appointment pages; no outcome guarantees; testimonials need consent.

## Dentists
- **Main action:** Book new-patient visit / emergency dental call.
- **Money page:** Implants, Invisalign, cosmetic, emergency dental — each its own landing page.
- **Trust:** Before/after smiles (consented), reviews, dentist bio, technology, financing (CareCredit), insurance list.
- **Form:** Name, phone, email, reason (checkbox list, not free text), insurance, preferred time.
- **Follow-up:** Online booking (NexHealth/LocalMed/etc.), reminder sequence, reactivation for unbooked leads.
- **Ask:** Most profitable procedure? New-patient special? Financing? Emergency slots? PMS (Dentrix/Open Dental/Eaglesoft)?
- **Compliance:** HIPAA as above; state dental board rules on "specialist" claims and advertised pricing.

## Lawyers / Law Firms
- **Main action:** Free case evaluation / consultation request · call.
- **Money page:** One page per practice area (and per city for PI).
- **Trust:** Bar admissions, years practicing, verdicts/settlements (with required disclaimers), Avvo/Super Lawyers, attorney photos, languages spoken.
- **Form:** Name, phone, email, case type (dropdown), short description (optional, warn not to share confidential details), preferred contact.
- **Follow-up:** Speed-to-lead critical (first firm to call often wins), intake qualification, conflict check, consult booking (Clio Grow/Lawmatics).
- **Ask:** Case types you want/don't want? Contingency or fee? Intake staff? After-hours answering? Minimum case value?
- **Compliance:** State bar advertising rules (ABA Model Rules 7.1–7.3): "past results do not guarantee" disclaimer, "specialist" restrictions, testimonials rules; "no attorney-client relationship formed by submitting this form".

## HVAC
- **Main action:** Call for service / book repair · secondary: request replacement estimate.
- **Money page:** AC/furnace replacement (high ticket), emergency repair, maintenance plan.
- **Trust:** License #, NATE certs, manufacturer dealer badges (Carrier/Trane), financing, reviews, trucks & techs photos, upfront pricing.
- **Form:** Name, phone, ZIP, service type (repair/replace/maintenance), system age, urgency.
- **Follow-up:** Missed-call text-back, dispatch integration (ServiceTitan/Housecall Pro), estimate follow-up sequence, maintenance-plan upsell.
- **Ask:** Seasonal peaks? Emergency 24/7? Financing partner? Service fee? Replacement vs repair margin?
- **Compliance:** License display where required; financing ad disclosures.

## Roofing
- **Main action:** Free roof inspection / estimate request.
- **Money page:** Storm damage / insurance claim page, full replacement page.
- **Trust:** Manufacturer certifications (GAF/Owens Corning), license & insurance, warranty, before/after, local project photos, reviews.
- **Form:** Name, phone, address, issue (leak/storm/replace), insurance claim yes/no, timing.
- **Follow-up:** Inspection scheduling, estimate follow-up (5–7 touches), review request after job.
- **Ask:** Residential vs commercial? Insurance claim help? Financing? Service radius? Storm season?
- **Compliance:** State rules on insurance-claim solicitation and deductible waivers — avoid "we pay your deductible".

## Plumbing
- **Main action:** Call now (emergency) / book service.
- **Money page:** Emergency plumbing, water heaters, drain/sewer, repiping.
- **Trust:** License #, upfront pricing, response time claim (only if true), reviews, uniformed techs.
- **Form:** Name, phone, ZIP, problem type, urgency.
- **Follow-up:** Instant callback, missed-call text-back, after-hours routing, estimate follow-up.
- **Ask:** 24/7? Response time? Trip fee? Most profitable job type? Dispatch software?
- **Compliance:** License display; don't advertise response times you can't hit.

## Car Shops / Auto Repair
- **Main action:** Book appointment / request quote · call.
- **Money page:** Brakes, diagnostics, transmission, fleet service, specific makes.
- **Trust:** ASE certification, warranty (e.g., 24k/24mo), reviews, shuttle/loaner, clean shop photos, makes serviced.
- **Form:** Name, phone, vehicle year/make/model, service needed, preferred date.
- **Follow-up:** Booking confirmation, reminder, digital inspection link, service-due reminders (retention).
- **Ask:** Makes you specialize in? Warranty? Loaners? Shop management software (Tekmetric/Shopmonkey)? Fleet accounts?
- **Compliance:** Estimate/authorization rules vary by state; don't publish fixed prices you won't honor.

## Movers & Packers
- **Main action:** Get a free moving quote.
- **Money page:** Local moves, long-distance, packing, commercial, storage.
- **Trust:** USDOT/MC number (interstate), insurance/valuation, reviews, crew photos, "no hidden fees" only if true.
- **Form:** Name, phone, email, move date, from ZIP, to ZIP, home size, packing needed. Consider multi-step form (feels shorter).
- **Follow-up:** Speed-to-lead (movers leads are shopped to 3–5 companies), virtual survey booking, quote follow-up, date-reminder.
- **Ask:** Local vs interstate? Peak season? Minimum job? Storage? Quote method (in-home/virtual/instant)?
- **Compliance:** FMCSA rules for interstate movers (USDOT display, "Your Rights and Responsibilities" booklet link); binding vs non-binding estimates language.

## Universal speed-to-lead note
For every vertical above, the first responder usually wins. If follow-up is slow, fixing that
often beats any design change — say so.
